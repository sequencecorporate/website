Privacy

If you contact us, we may collect your name, contact details, organisation and any information you choose to include in your enquiry. We may also receive personal information in the ordinary course of providing services or developing business relationships.

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, communicate with clients and prospective clients, provide our services, administer our business and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers that support the operation of our business and website, including email, IT and website-hosting providers. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including to maintain appropriate business records and meet legal, regulatory or professional requirements.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights over your personal information, including rights to access, correct, erase or restrict its use, and to object to certain processing.

If you have a question about how we use your information or want to exercise your rights, contact Kevin.Maguire@sequencecorporate.life.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
