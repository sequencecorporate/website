Solutions

Growth and Scaling

Growth often creates hidden regulatory, operational and governance challenges. Processes that worked perfectly well when there were five people in the business can start to break down when there are fifty.

We help management teams strengthen decision-making, accountability and operational resilience while maintaining commercial momentum. The objective is not to create unnecessary structure, but to ensure the business can continue to grow with confidence and that its maturity keeps pace with its scale.

Transactions and Investment Activity

Transactions create pressure. Deadlines tighten, information requests multiply and everybody suddenly wants answers at the same time.

We support organisations through investment, financing and transaction activity including preparation, negotiation strategy, due diligence and stakeholder management. We help with the difficult conversations, processes and decisions, but we also enjoy the detail that others often avoid – spreadsheets, documents, data and presenting your business in the best possible light.

Governance and Board Effectiveness

A "Board Pack" sounds intimidating; "Management Accounts" sounds worse! In reality, most businesses simply need accurate, proportionate information to make good decisions.

We help boards and management teams improve decision-making, accountability and oversight without creating unnecessary bureaucracy. Whether your reporting is too complex, too limited or simply inconsistent, we can help create an approach that works for your business and grows with it.

Regulatory and Compliance Challenges

Many businesses have policies, procedures or compliance requirements that started with good intentions but never quite reached the finish line.

Do you have a draft policy full of placeholders, gaps and unanswered questions? Are you unsure which requirements genuinely matter and which are creating unnecessary complexity?

We help organisations understand the jargon, identify the material risks and implement proportionate improvements. The aim is to focus effort where it creates value and only introduce requirements and controls that are necessary, workable and appropriate for your business.

Operational Resilience

Most organisations already know the issues that keep them awake at night.

A key supplier. A cyber incident. A critical employee. An operational dependency. A process that only one person understands.

We start there.

We help organisations strengthen resilience through practical improvements, leadership engagement and clear accountability. There are often simple actions that can be implemented quickly to reduce risk, improve confidence and keep the day-to-day business running smoothly.

Disputes and Special Situations

We support organisations through disputes, investigations and complex or contentious business situations requiring independent judgement and practical leadership. One of the most important questions in any dispute or difficult situation is understanding what success looks like.

Businesses often seek a moral victory, a legal victory and a commercial victory simultaneously. While all three can occasionally be achieved, doing so often requires significant time, cost and management attention.

We help clients identify the outcomes that matter most, make informed decisions and work effectively alongside legal advisers and other stakeholders. Effective dispute management involves understanding trade-offs, setting realistic objectives and adapting strategy as circumstances evolve. We speak complex languages including Boardroom, Legal and Commercial.
