About Kevin Maguire

Boardroom perspective. Practical delivery.

Kevin Maguire
Sequence Corporate

View Kevin on LinkedIn

Kevin Maguire is the person behind Sequence Corporate. A General Counsel, Company Secretary and Non-Executive Director, he brings 20 years’ experience across FTSE-listed companies, private businesses and SMEs, combining boardroom judgement, commercial breadth and detailed execution.

Working with Chairs, boards and executive teams through significant change, his remit extended across governance, legal strategy, financing and transactions, corporate risk and operational leadership, as well as restructuring, refinancing, IPO and listed-company life.

Through Sequence Corporate, owners, founders, boards and investors draw on the elements of that experience they need for their situation—from a difficult conversation or a specific corporate issue to a substantial transaction or period of change.

Often brought in when the problem or next step is unclear, or something important has landed in the ‘too hard’ box, Kevin helps identify what matters—and, just as importantly, what doesn’t—establish priorities, bring the right people together and take practical ownership of delivery.

His approach combines board-level judgement with the fine detail needed to put decisions into effect. Diplomatic, direct and practical, he bridges commercial imperatives, legal requirements and strategic objectives, delivering results without drama.

A big project isn’t always the right place to start. Good judgement includes knowing how much is enough.
